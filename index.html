// The Problem

Pet care in India is broken by fragmentation.

Every visit to a new vet starts from scratch. Vaccination records live on paper slips. Emergency decisions get made without history. The system fails pets — not because of lack of care, but lack of infrastructure.

📋

No Unified Health Record

Each vet visit is a fresh start. Critical history — allergies, past treatments, chronic conditions — gets repeated, misremembered, or lost.

🗺️

Fragmented Discovery

Pet parents spend precious time searching for trustworthy vets, groomers, and emergency care in moments of panic. No reliable system exists.

🔍

Zero Identity Infrastructure

Unlike humans, pets have no formal digital identity. They cannot be identified, verified, or tracked across providers — creating care gaps at every transition.

Four steps. One lifetime of care.

Register Your Pet

Create a free pet profile in under 2 minutes. Add basic details, breed, and a photo to generate a unique Pet Identity.
